    01 — Approach

    How I assess AI risk

    A repeatable loop that turns a model's behaviour into evidence a supervisor can act on.

    01

    Identify

    Map failure modes before they reach users: hallucination, reasoning collapse, out-of-distribution inputs and adversarial prompts.

    02

    Measure

    Design benchmarks and experiments, then quantify behaviour with per-class evaluation, confusion matrices and statistical testing.

    03

    Mitigate

    Put risk controls into production: trust-score abstention thresholds, human-in-the-loop routing, OOD detection and observability.

    04

    Communicate

    Write the findings up for technical and supervisory audiences, with governance documentation that supports accountable deployment.

    02 — Selected work

    Evaluation, safeguards & evidence

    Responsible AIRisk controls

    Agentic AI Evaluation Framework

    An agentic evaluation framework with built-in risk controls. Any response scoring below a 0.85 trust threshold makes the system abstain and route the case to a human reviewer. The safeguards had zero false positives in production, statistical analysis measured and reduced hallucination risk, and the full responsible AI governance documentation ships with the system.
